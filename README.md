# ORFan
R package. Scores the likelihood of any open reading frame (ORF) in alternative reading frames of a known gene to be accessed and translated by leaky ribosomal scanning.
Run in R 4.5.0 in RStudio v.2025.05.1+513 "Mariposa Orchid" for Windows

From De Angelis *et al*, 2026:

**The biochemical composition of coronavirus Nucleocapsid proteins drives the bicistronic properties of their sub-genomic mRNAs**

De Angelis, F., Chook, H. W., Apaa, T. T. *et al.* (2026). The biochemical composition of coronavirus Nucleocapsid proteins drives the bicistronic properties of their sub-genomic mRNAs. *BioRxiv* 2026.11.04.764307. https://doi.org/10.64898/2026.11.04.764307
